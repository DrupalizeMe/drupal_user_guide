## Contributing original writing

You should adapt or copy existing work (with appropriate license as
described below) whenever possible rather than writing completely new
text.

**When you contribute your original writing to this project, you agree
that it will be licensed under [CC BY-SA
2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).** To
recognize your contribution and acknowledge your copyright, provide your
name on the issue for the task you are submitting, and the project
managers will add it to the attributions information for the
Drupal User Guide. You may also add the name of an organization or company
that sponsored your work. You may turn both your name and your
organization/company name into links; the suggested link for your name
would be your Drupal.org user profile, but you may use a different URL
if you prefer.

Here are samples:

    Written by https://www.drupal.org/u/marysmith[Mary Smith].

    Written by https://www.drupal.org/u/marysmith[Mary Smith] of
    https://www.example.com[ABC Company].

## Adapting and including existing work

You are encouraged to include (or adapt) work that is licensed under
either the same license as this project, or compatible licenses. If you
do so, you must write up an attribution statement and add it to the
issue for the task you are working on. The statement must include:

- The title of the material.

- A link to the material, or if it is print material, publication
  information.

- Copyright notice (dates, creator, attribution parties).

- An indication of whether you modified the material (and your name for
  attribution if you did).

Here is a sample:

    Adapted by https://www.drupal.org/u/marysmith[Mary Smith] from
    https://www.drupal.org/node/14307["Documentation copyright and licensing"],
    copyright 2000-2026 by the individual contributors to the
    https://www.drupal.org/documentation[Drupal Community Documentation]

## Additional contributors

If you are editing work that has already been added to the guide, taking
up a task that was started and then dropped by another contributor, or
translating a topic, you’ll need to add your name to the existing
attribution. For instance:

    Written and edited by https://www.drupal.org/u/marysmith[Mary Smith] and
    https://www.drupal.org/u/joejones[Joe Jones]. Translated into Spanish by
    https://www.drupal.org/u/davidpaz[David Paz].
