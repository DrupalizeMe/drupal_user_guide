## Goal { #block-create-custom }

Create a block showing the hours and location of the farmers market.
## Prerequisite knowledge

* [block-concept](./block-concept.md)
* [block-regions](./block-regions.md)
## Steps

1. In the _Manage_ administrative menu, navigate to _Structure_ > _Block
    layout_ > _Custom block library_ (_admin/structure/block/block-content_).
1. Click _Add custom block_. The _Add custom block_ page appears.
1. Fill in the fields as shown below.
    <table>
      <thead>
        <tr>
          <th>Field name</th>
          <th>Explanation</th>
          <th>Example value</th>
        </tr>
      </thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td>Block description</td>
          <td>Name of the block shown to administrators</td>
          <td>Hours and location block</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>Body</td>
          <td>Content of the block when it is displayed</td>
          <td>Open: Sundays, 9 AM to</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>

    ![Add a custom block](/images/block-create-custom-add-custom-block.png)

1. Click _Save_. A message appears indicating the block has been saved.
## Expand your understanding

* Edit the content of your custom block. In the _Manage_ administrative menu,
navigate to _Structure_ > _Block layout_ > _Custom block library_
(_admin/structure/block/block-content_). Find your block in the list and click
_Edit_ to make changes.
* Place the block you created in the sidebar. See [block-place](./block-place.md) for details.
