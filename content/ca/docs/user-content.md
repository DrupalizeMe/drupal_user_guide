## Goal { #user-content }

Assign Vendor content items Happy Farm and Sweet Honey to the corresponding
Vendor user accounts, so they can edit their own Vendor profiles on the site.
## Prerequisite knowledge

* [user-concept](./user-concept.md)
## Site prerequisites

* The Vendor content type must exist, and your site must have at least two
Vendor content items. See [structure-content-type](./structure-content-type.md), [structure-fields](./structure-fields.md), and
[content-create](./content-create.md).
* User accounts for at least two vendors must exist. See [user-new-user](./user-new-user.md).
## Steps

1. In the _Manage_ administrative menu, navigate to _Content_ (_admin/content_).
1. Find Vendor content item Happy Farm in the list. If it is not immediately
    visible, you can filter the list by _Published status_, _Type_ (Vendor), _Title_
    or _Language_.  Click _Edit_ for the Vendor content item you would like to
    assign an author to.
1. Locate the information block and click _Authoring information_.
    ![Assigning an author to the Vendor content item](/images/user-content.png)
1. Start typing the Vendor's user name Happy Farm in the _Authored by_ field.
    The field lists matching user names. Select the Vendor's user name from the
    list.
1. Click _Save and keep published_.
1. You will get a notification that the Vendor content item has been updated.
    ![Vendor content item has been updated](/images/user-content_updated.png)
1. Follow these steps again to assign Vendor content item Sweet Honey to the
    Vendor user account Sweet Honey.
